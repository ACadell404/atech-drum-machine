In this window

Click or drag across the grid to paint steps. Click a drum's badge to hear it, −/+ to set its velocity. Space play/stop, 1–6 pads, T tap tempo.

On the board

Step knob moves along the steps, press to toggle. Drum knob picks the drum, press to hear it. The screen and this page stay in sync.

Holding Shift on the board

Step knob sets tempo, drum knob sets the drum's velocity. Press step knob to clear the row, drum knob to load the demo beat.
